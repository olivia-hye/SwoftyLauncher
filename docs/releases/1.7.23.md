# 1.7.23

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.7.23 stops special liveries on addon cars from putting the drivers in another team's suit.

## Addons

- Picking a special livery in Team Select, such as Lone Star Bull or the pink Alpine in the Classic Seasons 2023 box, no longer dresses the drivers in McLaren's 2025 suit. A special livery for a car with no livery row of its own in the game used to take the suit of the game's first livery; it now leaves the suit to the drivers' own kit, as the car's standard livery does. This affected every special livery in the Classic Seasons 2021 to 2024 boxes.
