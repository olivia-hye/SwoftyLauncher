# 1.8.5

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.8.5 shows the in-game overlay (addon panels, toasts, and the keyboard and mouse focus they take) on AMD cards with frame generation off.

## Overlay

- F1 25 creates its picture through AMD FidelityFX whatever upscaler is chosen. The overlay treated that as frame generation and drew nothing, so on AMD cards with TAA or FSR and frame generation off, addons showed no panels or toasts. It now draws while frame generation is off in the game's settings, and stops when it's turned on. Thanks to Nitrocosm.
