# 1.7.25

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.7.25 lets you make room for more car and season addons by turning one off, and stops a false "Career is Missing" notice after My Team sessions.

## Addons

- When your season and car addons fill the game's table of car packages, Play stops with "vehicle_packages has no free stat_id up to 254". Turning off a season or car addon you aren't using now makes room for the others. Before, the launcher kept that addon's car ids reserved, so the error stayed until the addon named in it was turned off. Ids a career uses stay with that career.

## Saves

- Continuing a My Team career no longer shows "1 Career is Missing" on the Saves page after you close F1 25. The career was never missing: the launcher read it under two names and reported the one it wasn't using. If you saw this notice, don't restore for it; your current save is the right one.

## Downloads

- Addon and launcher downloads read in larger chunks and keep a deeper buffer between the network and the disk, so a slow disk or antivirus scan no longer holds up the connection mid-download.
