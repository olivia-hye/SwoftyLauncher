# 1.7.21

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.7.21 shows every installed addon on your profiles' Addons page again, and stops a crash as a session loads when your car no longer exists in the game.

## Crashes

- A session no longer crashes as it loads when your car isn't among the session's cars. This happened when continuing a Grand Prix or career saved before Classic Seasons 0.7.0 gave its cars fixed ids, and in a Classic Career with some of the Classic Seasons years it uses not installed. The AI then builds that session with the first team's performance curves, and `[ai] team curves` in `loader.log` notes it once. Your own car can still be the wrong one in such a save; a new Grand Prix or career gets the right one. `SWOFTY_AI_CURVES_GUARD=0` turns the guard off.

## Launcher

- Profiles you had before 1.7.20 list every installed addon again. 1.7.20 showed only the addons a profile had already turned on or moved, so the others seemed to have gone, although they stayed installed. On its first start 1.7.21 adds each installed addon a profile doesn't list to the end of that profile, turned off; the ones it already lists keep their order and switches. `launcher-app.log` notes how many it added to each profile ([adding an addon to a profile](/using/addons#add-an-addon-to-a-profile)).
- When an addon you install can't be added to the active profile, `launcher-app.log` says why.
