# 1.8.6

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.8.6 lets addons change how each AI car races while a session runs: how far back it defends, when it starts and gives up an overtake, when it pulls out of a queue, and how far off the racing line it may stay into a corner.

## Addons

- 48 of the AI's driver settings are live car fields: `ai.blocking.*`, `ai.overtake.*`, `ai.space_seeking.*`, `ai.offset_variation.*` and `ai.alongside.*`. A layer changes one car for as long as it is held and goes away with it. The game's own values are never written, so every other car and the next session keep theirs. See [AI](/std/systems/ai#racecraft-per-car-live).
- Lap 1 Realistic AI, in the Addon Browser, is the first addon to use them: on lap 1 the AI brakes later, goes for the inside instead of queueing, stays side by side into corners and defends from further back.
